# How to Find Reliable Pet Boarding for the Holidays

The holiday season brings joy, excitement, and plenty of travel plans, but for pet owners, it also means finding the right care for their beloved furry companions. Whether you're going on a Christmas vacation or taking a New Year's getaway, it's essential to ensure your pet is well cared for while you're away.

With many options available, it can be daunting to find a boarding facility that fits your pet's needs. In this guide, we’ll walk you through how to find reliable pet boarding during the holidays and ensure your pet is in safe hands.

---

## 1. Plan Ahead and Book Early

During peak holiday seasons like Christmas and New Year, pet boarding facilities are often in high demand. Therefore, it’s crucial to start your search early. Popular pet boarding centres fill up fast, and waiting until the last minute could mean your pet misses out on a spot. By booking early, you give yourself enough time to research options, visit facilities, and secure a reservation for your pet’s stay. Some facilities even offer early-bird discounts, so it's a win-win to get ahead of the rush.

---

## 2. Consider Your Pet’s Individual Needs

Every pet is unique, and what works for one animal might not work for another. When searching for a pet boarding facility, consider your pet’s specific needs. Does your pet have dietary restrictions, health concerns, or a preference for a particular sleeping environment? Make sure the boarding facility can accommodate these requirements. For example, if your dog requires medication or has special health needs, find a facility with staff trained to handle such situations. Additionally, if your pet is elderly, anxious, or doesn’t get along well with other animals, look for a facility that provides quiet, less crowded environments.

---

## 3. Check Vaccination Requirements

Most reputable pet boarding facilities will require that your pet is up-to-date on their vaccinations before staying. This is crucial for your pet’s health and the well-being of other animals in the facility. Check with the boarding facility about their vaccination policy well in advance, ensuring your pet’s shots are current. Common vaccinations that are required include rabies, Bordetella (kennel cough), and DHPP (distemper, hepatitis, parvovirus, and parainfluenza). Bringing your pet’s vaccination records along when you drop them off will help expedite the check-in process.

---

## 4. Drop by and Visit the Facility Before You Book Your Pet’s Stay

It’s always a good idea to visit the pet boarding facility in person before making a reservation. This gives you a chance to assess the cleanliness, safety, and general environment where your pet will be staying. Look for secure gates, proper ventilation, and a comfortable atmosphere. Speak with the staff to get a feel for how they interact with the animals and how knowledgeable they are about pet care. This visit will provide peace of mind that your pet will be in a safe and caring environment while you’re away.

---

## 5. Research Reputable Boarding Options

It’s vital to choose a pet boarding facility that has a solid reputation for providing excellent care. Start by reading reviews on trusted websites like Yelp, Google Reviews, or platforms that specialize in pet services. Positive reviews from other pet owners can help you narrow down your choices. Make sure the facility is licensed and insured, as this offers additional protection for both you and your pet.

In addition to reading reviews, consider visiting the boarding facilities in person. This gives you an opportunity to inspect the cleanliness, safety, and overall environment of the space where your pet will stay. A reputable facility should be clean, safe, and well-maintained, with secure gates, ventilation, and appropriate lighting.

---

## 6. Look for Personalized Care and Attention

During the holidays, many pets are boarding for longer durations, and it’s important that they receive personalized care to ensure they’re comfortable and happy. Look for pet boarding facilities that offer individualized care plans. Some facilities assign staff members to care for specific pets, ensuring your pet gets the attention they need. Personalized care can make a significant difference, especially if your pet is nervous or requires extra TLC. Ask if they offer one-on-one playtime, grooming sessions, or enrichment activities to keep your pet mentally and physically stimulated.

---

## 7. Check Safety Standards and Cleanliness

Safety should be your number one priority when choosing a pet boarding facility. Check the boarding environment for safety hazards such as unsecured fencing, exposed wires, or dangerous chemicals. Ensure the facility has emergency protocols in place and that staff is trained to handle emergency situations, from health issues to natural disasters.

Cleanliness is also crucial to preventing the spread of diseases. The boarding space should be regularly cleaned, and pets should have access to clean bedding and fresh water. Ask the facility about their cleaning practices and how often they disinfect common areas. A trustworthy boarding facility will prioritize hygiene and cleanliness, ensuring your pet stays healthy during their stay.

---

## 8. Confirm Everything a Week Before Your Pet’s Stay

A week before your pet’s scheduled boarding stay, confirm all details with the facility. This includes confirming drop-off and pick-up times, ensuring your pet’s vaccinations are up to date, and double-checking any special instructions for their care. Reconfirm your pet’s reservation to avoid any miscommunication and to ensure everything is in place for your pet’s arrival.

---

## 9. Understand Socialization Policies

If your pet is social and enjoys interacting with other animals, a boarding facility that offers group playtime or socialization sessions might be ideal. However, not all pets are comfortable with this setup. If your pet is more reserved or tends to be aggressive toward other animals, make sure the facility provides individual spaces for rest and play. Inquire about how the facility monitors interactions between pets and how they prevent conflicts during group activities.

---

## 10. Ask About Communication During Your Pet’s Stay

Being away from your pet during the holidays can be emotionally difficult, but many boarding facilities offer ways to keep you updated on how your pet is doing. Ask if the facility offers regular updates through pictures, video calls, or daily reports. This can provide reassurance that your pet is well cared for and help you stay connected to your furry companion, even when you're miles away.

---
